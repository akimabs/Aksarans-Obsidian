# PvP Mind Games

## Knowledge layers

In PvP, players should know:

- opponent's five available creature,
- what each creature generally does,
- previous sequences the opponent favored.

They should not know the full current action before committing their own.

## Example

Opponent party:

```text
HA NA CA RA KA
```

Opponent reveals/telegraphs:

```text
HA + ? + ?
```

You know HA begins the action, but you must estimate:

- attack + Break?
- attack + Guard?
- attack + Counter?
- fast short combo?
- slow long combo?

## Desired skill

PvP skill comes from:

1. knowing creature functions,
2. remembering opponent tendencies,
3. risk management,
4. sequence construction,
5. occasional Tembung optimization.

Not from memorizing a fixed elemental weakness chart.
