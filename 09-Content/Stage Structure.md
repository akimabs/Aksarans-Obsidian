# Stage Structure

No exploration system is required.

Simple PvE shell:

```text
Chapter
|- Battle 1
|- Battle 2
|- Battle 3
`- Boss
```

A stage can exist for one of three reasons:

1. teach a mechanic,
2. test a mechanic,
3. reward progression.

Avoid filler stages whose only difference is larger HP.

## Example teaching order

1. Basic Attack and Guard.
2. Introduce Break.
3. Introduce Charge.
4. Introduce speed/long combo risk.
5. Boss mixes them.
