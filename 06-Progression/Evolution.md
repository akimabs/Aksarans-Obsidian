# Evolution

Each of the 20 creature has Evolution I-III.

## Evo I

Introduces the creature's Core and Link identity.

## Evo II

Strengthens that identity.

Prefer clearer/stronger values or a modest extension rather than adding a completely new subsystem.

## Evo III

Unlocks a **Mastery Trait**.

Examples of the pattern, not final abilities:

- "If CA is the last slot, Break also applies Vulnerable."
- "If RA is slot 3+, sequence gains additional speed."
- "If KA follows a defensive Core, counter becomes stronger."

This makes slot order matter more at endgame.

## Design goal

Player should think:

> "My old combo just gained a new use."

not:

> "Everything I learned before evolution is obsolete."
