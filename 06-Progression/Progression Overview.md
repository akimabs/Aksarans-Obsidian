# Progression Overview

Progression has two axes.

## Horizontal progression: collection

Unlock new aksara creature.

```text
HA NA CA RA KA
      + DA
      + TA
      + ...
```

Each new creature adds a new command to possible party compositions.

## Vertical progression: evolution

```text
HA I -> HA II -> HA III
```

Creature becomes better and more expressive without invalidating what player learned.

## Recommended start

- Show all 20 silhouettes/entries in Dex.
- Start with roughly 5 owned creature.
- Unlock the rest gradually.

Exact order is open.

## What progression should NOT require yet

- equipment rarity ladders,
- crafting trees,
- dozens of currencies.

Core progression should first prove that collection + evolution alone feels rewarding.
