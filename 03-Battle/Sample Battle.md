# Sample Battle

> [!warning]
> All numbers and abilities are illustrative only.

Party: HA, NA, CA, RA, KA.

Enemy: Stone Warden.

## Turn 1

Enemy Intent: Attack.

Player chooses `NA + KA`.

- NA Core -> Guard.
- KA Link -> Counter.

Result: reduce incoming hit and retaliate.

## Turn 2

Enemy Intent: Guard.

Player chooses `CA + HA`.

- CA Core -> Break.
- HA Link -> additional force.

Result: efficient damage into Guard.

## Turn 3

Enemy Intent: Charge.

Player chooses `HA + CA + RA`.

- HA Core -> attack.
- CA Link -> Break.
- RA Link -> speed up.

Result: fast Break interrupts or heavily punishes Charge.

## Turn 4

Enemy Intent: Heavy Attack.

Player chooses `HA + NA + KA`.

Attack + partial Guard + Counter.

## Turn 5

Enemy Intent: Fortify.

Player chooses `HA + NA + CA + RA`.

Long sequence gives attack + guard + Break, while RA compensates some speed cost.

## What this battle should teach

- Order matters.
- Different enemy states demand different answers.
- Combo 4 is not always the default best action.
- Five party members are a toolbox, not five interchangeable attackers.
