# Resolution and Speed

## Current idea

Sequence length gives a default speed:

- 2 = Fast
- 3 = Normal
- 4 = Slow

Creature effects can modify speed.

## Purpose

Speed exists to create a cost for long combos and to enable decisions such as:

> "Do I use a powerful 4-slot action and risk getting hit first, or answer quickly with 2 slots?"

## Not yet decided

- Exact initiative formula.
- Whether Fast always beats Normal/Slow or uses numeric initiative.
- What happens on ties.
- Whether an enemy action can be fully interrupted or only weakened.
- How PvP resolves two simultaneous counters.

Keep this simple in prototype.
