# PvE Rules

## Purpose of PvE

PvE teaches the combat language and gives progression goals.

## Enemy Intent

PvE currently uses **full or highly readable intent** before player locks a sequence.

Examples:

- Attack
- Guard
- Charge
- Special

Intent should preferably be readable through both:

1. icon / stance / animation,
2. optional text for new players.

Example:

```text
      [enemy]
         shield icon
        GUARD
```

## Why reveal intent

Without readable intent, a counter-driven PvE battle can collapse into guessing. PvE should teach:

> "Guard -> I should think about Break."

Later enemies can become harder through:

- mixed patterns,
- delayed actions,
- incomplete information on specific encounters,
- changing states,

without hiding everything from the beginning.

## Scope

No explore/map required.

Recommended content shell:

```text
Stage 1 -> Stage 2 -> Stage 3 -> Boss
```

Stage selection can be a simple list or chapter path UI.
