# Enemy Design

## Enemy job

An enemy exists to ask a combat question.

Examples:

- Aggressive enemy tests Guard/Tanggah.
- Armored enemy tests Break.
- Charging enemy tests speed/interrupt.
- Mixed enemy tests prediction and flexible party composition.

## Avoid

Enemy difficulty should not primarily come from inflated HP.

## Telegraph

Readable PvE telegraph can use:

- pose change,
- symbol over head,
- short label,
- animation wind-up.

## Progression

Early enemies teach one property at a time.

Later enemies combine properties or change behavior after being countered.
